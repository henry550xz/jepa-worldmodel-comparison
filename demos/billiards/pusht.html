READ THE RESULTS IN CONTEXT

Three distinctions that matter.

Layer taps ≠ shallow models

A block-4 tap belongs to a model trained with twelve blocks. A depth-4 encoder is trained from scratch with only four. The independent-depth study includes Gaussian and Sparse; no Pixel depth sweep was run.

Ranking ≠ goal completion

Candidate Spearman measures action ordering. Closed-loop success depends on repeated decisions and retaining a reached goal. These custom physical-goal scores are distinct from official PushT reproduction scores.

Probe accuracy ≠ all information

A frozen probe measures accessibility under its fitting budget. It does not prove that other information is absent, nor that a physical coordinate is uniquely causal.

METHOD & PROVENANCE

Evidence with its boundaries intact.

Layer probes use 512 / 128 / 124 disjoint train / validation / test episodes. Small dynamics predictors are separately fitted on frozen features; they are not the original trained AdaLN predictor. H32 uses only four episodes in those evaluations.

Visited-state test results use 12 seed clusters and 144 banks, with 64 candidates per bank. Pointwise bootstrap intervals are exploratory, not corrected for multiple comparisons and not estimates of training-seed uncertainty. Original intervention controls match rank; later token interventions additionally match displacement magnitude.

Appearance and intervention panels have their own sample sets and definitions; compare within a panel. Lines show descriptive averages of selected table rows. Error bars appear only where the source supplies the corresponding interval.
